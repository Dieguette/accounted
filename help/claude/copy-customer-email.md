# Copy a customer's email address

You can copy a customer's email address with one click, so you can paste it into a quote, a chat message or another tool. You do not need to select the text by hand.

## Copy the address

1. In the menu, go to **Invoicing > Customers**.
2. Click the customer you want to open.
3. In the **Contact details** section, find the **Email** row.
4. Click the small copy icon right after the email address.

The address is now on your clipboard. Paste it where you need it with Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on a Mac).

If you hover over the icon, it shows the label **Copy email address**.

## What you will see

When the copy works, a message appears in the top right corner of the screen: **Email address copied**. The message disappears on its own.

You can click the icon again at any time to copy the address again.

## If the copy does not work

If a red message says **Could not copy the email address**, your browser did not allow access to the clipboard. The address is still shown on the page. Select the text and copy it yourself with Ctrl+C (Cmd+C on a Mac).

## If there is no copy icon

The icon only appears when the customer has an email address. If the **Email** row shows a dash instead of an address, nothing is saved for that customer yet.

To add an email address, click **Edit** at the top right of the customer page, fill in the **Email** field and save. You need permission to edit customers to do this.

## Good to know

- Clicking the email address itself still opens your mail program, as before.
- Copying the address does not change anything on the customer.
- Everyone who can open the customer sees the copy icon, including users with read-only access.
- If you use Accounted in Swedish, the icon label is **Kopiera e-postadressen**, and the messages are **E-postadressen kopierades** and **Kunde inte kopiera e-postadressen**.
