# Copy a customer's email address or phone number

You can copy a customer's email address or phone number with one click, so you can paste it into a quote, a chat message or another tool. You do not need to select the text by hand.

## Copy the address or number

1. In the menu, go to **Invoicing > Customers**.
2. Click the customer you want to open.
3. In the **Contact details** section, find the **Email** row or the **Phone** row.
4. Click the small copy icon right after the email address or the phone number.

The address or number is now on your clipboard. Paste it where you need it with Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on a Mac).

If you hover over the icon, it shows the label **Copy email address** or **Copy phone number**.

## What you will see

When the copy works, a message appears in the top right corner of the screen: **Email address copied** or **Phone number copied**. The message disappears on its own.

You can click the icon again at any time to copy again.

## If the copy does not work

If a red message says **Could not copy the email address** or **Could not copy the phone number**, your browser did not allow access to the clipboard. The address or number is still shown on the page. Select the text and copy it yourself with Ctrl+C (Cmd+C on a Mac).

## If there is no copy icon

Each icon only appears when that detail is saved. If the **Email** row or the **Phone** row shows a dash, nothing is saved for that detail yet, and there is no icon in that row.

To add an email address or a phone number, click **Edit** at the top right of the customer page, fill in the **Email** or **Phone** field and save. You need permission to edit customers to do this.

## Good to know

- Clicking the email address itself still opens your mail program, as before. The phone number is plain text and does not start a call.
- Copying does not change anything on the customer.
- Everyone who can open the customer sees the copy icons, including users with read-only access.
- If you use Accounted in Swedish, the icon labels are **Kopiera e-postadressen** and **Kopiera telefonnumret**. The messages are **E-postadressen kopierades**, **Telefonnumret kopierades**, **Kunde inte kopiera e-postadressen** and **Kunde inte kopiera telefonnumret**.
