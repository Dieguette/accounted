You can copy a customer's email address straight from their customer page, so you can paste it into your email program, a message, or another document without typing it.

## Copy the email address

1. Go to **Customers** and open the customer you want.
2. Find the **Contact details** section.
3. Next to the customer's email address, click the copy icon (two overlapping squares). If you hover over it, it says **Copy email address**.
4. Paste the address wherever you need it.

<Tip>
  Clicking the email address itself opens a new email to the customer in your email program. Use the copy icon when you only want the address.
</Tip>

## When it works

A short message appears that says **Email address copied**. The address is now ready to paste.

## If copying fails

If your browser does not allow Accounted to copy, a red message appears that says **Could not copy the email address**. Nothing is copied.

This can happen if your browser blocks copying or if the Accounted window was not active when you clicked. To get the address anyway:

- Click the copy icon again.
- Or select the email address on the page with your mouse and copy it the usual way (for example, **Ctrl+C** on Windows or **Cmd+C** on Mac).

## If the customer has no email address

If no email address is saved for the customer, you see a dash (**–**) next to **Email** and there is no copy icon.

To add an email address, click **Edit** at the top of the customer page, fill in the email, and save. The copy icon then appears next to the address.

<Note>
  If you only have viewer access in the company, the **Edit** button is locked. Ask someone with edit access to add the email address.
</Note>
